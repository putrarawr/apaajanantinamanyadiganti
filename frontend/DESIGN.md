## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes the robust, community-driven listing and peer-to-peer commerce utilities of Carousell with the refined, tactile minimalism of Apple’s consumer hardware showcase pages. It addresses the common pitfall of peer-to-peer marketplaces—visual clutter, cognitive overload, and chaotic content density—by framing everyday user listings within an immaculate, museum-grade container.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on **Minimalist High-Fidelity Modernism**:
- **Clarity over density:** High visual breathing room, deliberate whitespace, and structured modularity replace overcrowded multi-column grids.
- **Subtle physical authenticity:** Translucent frosted glass layers, micro-radii card surfaces, and whisper-soft ambient drop shadows evoke premium physical devices and polished retail experiences.
- **Editorial authority:** Clean sans-serif hierarchy presents peer-to-peer merchandise with the visual respect, lighting, and restraint traditionally reserved for flagship industrial design.

The design targets discerning digital consumers who appreciate frictionless discovery, verified peer transparency, and quiet luxury in everyday commerce. The emotional response is one of instant trust, calm focus, and effortless navigation.

## Colors

The palette adheres strictly to the classic Cupertino chromatic hierarchy, prioritizing content clarity over decorative tinting:

- **Primary (`#0071E3`)**: Apple Signature Blue. Dedicated exclusively to interactive purchase pathways, primary action buttons, active pill states, and trust verification highlights.
- **Secondary (`#1D1D1F`)**: Deep Apple Dark Charcoal. Anchors all primary typographic hierarchy, high-contrast badges, and selected navigation states.
- **Tertiary (`#86868B`)**: Apple Neutral Grey. Provides balanced legibility for secondary metadata, product condition descriptors, timestamps, and subtle UI strokes.
- **Neutral (`#F5F5F7`)**: Apple Soft Grey canvas. Establishes the foundational canvas background, generating soft, eye-resting contrast against crisp pure white content containers.
- **Surface & Functional Colors**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) is reserved for elevated product tiles and bento containers. Success (`#34C759`), warning (`#FF9500`), and alert tones (`#FF3B30`) adhere to system standards, applied minimally to seller credibility tags and deal states.

## Typography

The typographic architecture mirrors the precision of Apple’s human interface guidelines using Inter. 

- **Display & Headings:** Rendered with tight negative letter tracking (`-0.025em` to `-0.015em`) and high contrast weights (600 and 700). Headings deliver concise, confident statements with generous leading.
- **Body & Product Metadata:** Scaled deliberately around the 15px standard for general titles and 13px for contextual attributes (location, condition, age).
- **Price Architecture:** Price indicators utilize a distinct weight (`price-display`), set slightly bolder than adjacent textual copy to guarantee instant visual acquisition without aggressive badge colors.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Crisp 11px to 14px sizes with neutral-to-positive tracking, keeping timestamps, verification pips, and chip tags razor-sharp across both standard and high-density screens.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout shifts marketplace conventions away from dense, edge-to-edge listings into a controlled, breathing showcase.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width container of `1280px` centered within the viewport. Product listings map to a spacious 4-column fluid grid governed by `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters. Feature callouts adopt a multi-span bento framework (2-column and 3-column compositions).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Collapses to a balanced 3-column product grid with `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters and `2rem` page margins.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** Adapts to a 2-column card grid or 1-column modular list view with `0.75rem` (`12px`) gutters and `1rem` outer canvas padding.

### Spacing Philosophy
Consistent multiples of 4px and 8px govern internal card padding and section rhythms:
- Section gaps transition with significant whitespace (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`) to demarcate editorial discovery, bento highlights, and core product feeds.
- Product card interiors use a strict `space-md` (`16px`) padding envelope around photographic assets and contextual data clusters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent optical filtration and extra-diffused ambient illumination, avoiding hard borders or heavy drops:

- **Glassmorphic Sticky Nav:** Sticky top headers and floating modal bars utilize a `surface-glass` treatment (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`) backed by a high-radius hardware blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px); -webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), allowing listings to glide subtly beneath the chrome.
- **Card Ambient Elevation:** Product cards sit flush or near-flush to the `#F5F5F7` ground canvas, lifted by a soft ambient shadow: `0px 4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Active & Hover Elevation:** Interactive tiles scale up imperceptibly (`scale(1.015)`) while lifting with an expanded ambient bloom: `0px 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.07)`.
- **Modal & Floating Tooling:** Overlays and quick-preview sheets utilize deep ambient diffusion: `0px 24px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs consistent continuous-curve styling (`squircle`-inspired visual geometry):

- **Product & Bento Cards:** Constructed with a generous `16px` to `20px` border radius (`rounded-2xl`), giving each listing the feel of an Apple hardware tile.
- **Pills & Interactive Selectors:** Buttons, search fields, category chips, and filter toggles utilize full pill styling (`rounded-full` / `9999px`), ensuring tactile touch targets.
- **Image Frames:** Product photo containers internal to cards maintain an inset radius of `12px` to `14px`, keeping border margins harmonious with the outer container.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary CTA:** Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`), filled with `#0071E3`, white label (`label-md`), featuring a subtle inner border highlight and no harsh exterior drop shadow.
- **Secondary / Action Pill:** Subtle grey background (`#E8E8ED`) with `#1D1D1F` text; shifts to pure `#1D1D1F` with white text when activated.
- **Filter Chips:** Pill containers with 8px horizontal padding, featuring subtle micro-icons (e.g., condition, price tier, delivery method).

### Product Cards
- **Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface, `16px` radius, `0 4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)` elevation, no border strokes.
- **Image Showcase:** 1:1 square aspect ratio image resting on a soft tinted base (`#FBFBFD`) to maintain visual fidelity even with transparent or white background product photos.
- **Seller Meta Inset:** Embedded seller avatar (24px circle) positioned at the upper corner or directly above the title, accompanied by a verified check icon in `#0071E3`.
- **Information Cluster:** Product Title capped at 2 lines with ellipsis, followed immediately by bold price presentation (`price-display`), ending with muted condition and geographic metadata (`#86868B`, 13px).

### Bento Feature Cards
- **Structure:** Modular feature blocks (2x1 and 1x1 aspect ratios) highlighting buyer protection, authentication guarantees, and courier partnerships.
- **Visuals:** Minimalist line iconography, oversized typographic anchors, and generous internal padding (`32px`).

### Input Fields & Search Navigation
- **Universal Search:** Pill-shaped container (`44px` height) with `#E8E8ED` background (focus state expands slightly with white background and `#0071E3` hairline stroke). Contains muted placeholder text: *"Search beauty, tech, curated pre-loved..."*.

### Footers & Footnotes
- **Architecture:** Multi-column text links rendered in muted grey (`#86868B`, 12px) over `#F5F5F7`, with subtle accordion collapsible tiers on mobile viewports.
